PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: 2 October 2026 · Updated: 3 October 2026 (profile photo, in-app deletion, crash reports, reports and blocks) · Applies to the Patang Wars app for Android.

In short

Patang Wars keeps only what it needs to save your game and let you play online. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
A random account IDWhen you play as a guestTo save your progress in the cloud without asking who you are.
Your Google name, email address, profile picture link and Google account IDOnly if you choose “Sign in with Google”To sign you in and bring your progress back on a new phone. Your email is never shown to other players.
Game progress: player name you choose, coins, gems, XP and level, kites, dor and themes you own, rank, season badges, match results and countsWhile you playTo save and restore your game and to apply the game’s rules on our server (so progress can’t be faked).
Online match records: the two player IDs, who won, how the match ended, number of pechay, match lengthAfter each online matchTo update ranks fairly, stop cheating and win-trading, and build the season leaderboard.
A profile photo you choose (optional)Only if you pick one in ProfileShown on your own profile. We keep a small square copy (256×256) in private storage so it comes back on a new phone. For now only you can see it.
Reports and blocks: who reported or blocked whom, the reason you chose, the match and the player name at that timeOnly if you use REPORT / BLOCK after an online matchTo stop abuse and cheating, and so blocked players are never matched with you again. Reports are kept for at most a year.
Crash reports: the error message and where in the app it happened, the app version, your phone model and Android versionWhen the app hits an errorTo find and fix bugs. Not linked to your account or name; kept for at most 90 days.
A random device IDWhen the app syncsSo the same game event is never counted twice.
Your IP address and connection dataWhile connected to our serversNeeded to send data over the internet; kept only in short-lived server logs for security and fixing problems.

We do not collect your contacts, location, microphone, camera, phone number or payment card details, and the app never looks through your gallery — the only photo we receive is the one you pick yourself as your profile photo. The app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or crash services at this time — crash reports go only to our own database.

What other players can see

In online matches and on the leaderboard, other players can see your player name, rank, the kite and dor you fly, and your win record. They never see your email or Google account.

Who processes the data

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in and file storage service, including profile photos (servers in Mumbai, India).
  • Oracle Cloud — our online game server (servers in Dubai, UAE).
  • Google — only if you use “Sign in with Google”, under Google’s own privacy policy.

These companies process data only to run the game for us. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Your account and progress are kept while your account exists. Short-lived server logs are deleted automatically within 30 days, and crash reports within 90 days. If you delete your account, your profile, progress, profile photo, sign-in details, the online match records that include you, and the reports and blocks you made or received are deleted. You can remove your profile photo yourself any time in Profile → Photo hatao.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Account → ACCOUNT MITAO, type MITAO — your account and all its data are deleted immediately. Full steps and what is deleted.

Without the app: email aakashbaloch2125@gmail.com with the subject “Patang Wars account deletion” and your player name or the ID shown in Profile → Account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also wipe your progress any time in Settings → Reset.

Children

Patang Wars is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS / secure WebSocket). Players can only read their own saved profile; only our server can change it.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example when in-app purchases arrive), we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
